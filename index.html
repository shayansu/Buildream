FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

You Want To Ask Something From Us?

Have questions about your construction project? Find answers to some of our most common questions.

What are the problems in construction?

The usual problems are delays, budget overruns and unclear plans. We agree on scope, schedule and pricing before work starts.

What is best practice in construction?

Plan thoroughly, use certified materials, maintain site safety and keep the client informed throughout the project.

How long does a typical project take?

Small renovations can take two to four weeks, while larger construction projects can take several months.
